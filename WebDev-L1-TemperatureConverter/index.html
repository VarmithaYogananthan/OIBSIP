    Behind the numbers: formulas & precision

    First, convert to Kelvin.
    K = °C + 273.15
    K = (°F + 459.67) × 5/9

    Then, choose your scale.
    °C = K − 273.15
    °F = K × 9/5 − 459.67

    Values below absolute zero are rejected. No intermediate rounding is applied. Results show up to six decimal places; very small or large values use scientific notation. JavaScript floating-point arithmetic can introduce tiny rounding differences.
