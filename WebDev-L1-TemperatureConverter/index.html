How the conversion works

First convert to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15, or K = (°F + 459.67) × 5/9. Then °C = K − 273.15 and °F = K × 9/5 − 459.67.

Values below absolute zero are rejected. Results show up to six decimal places; very small or large values use scientific notation. Floating-point arithmetic can introduce tiny rounding differences.
